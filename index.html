How it works

Live on your WhatsApp number in three steps

No new app for parents. They message the number they already use, and Ahlify replies.

  1. Share your information

    Send us your fee schedule, admissions policy and common questions. We set up the assistant around them.

  2. Connect WhatsApp Business

    We link Ahlify to your school's WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official platform.

  3. Your team stays in control

    Ahlify answers routine questions and hands serious enquiries and tour requests to your admissions staff.
